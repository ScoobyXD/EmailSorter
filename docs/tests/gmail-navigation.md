1. Dock classifies emails and applies its owned Gmail labels only after an explicit Categorize emails action.
2. Clicking a Dock category opens its label in Gmail's native message list.
3. Category clicks do not fetch email details or call Jev.
4. Dock reads label names once when the Gmail workspace loads and after account connection or category import.
5. Saved label IDs resolve the correct labels, including older Dock labels with hashed names.
6. A category without a Gmail label shows a clear notice and does not open an unrelated label.
7. Uncategorized opens only the label for emails explicitly classified without a matching category.
8. Native label views include labeled mail outside the latest categorization window and outside Inbox.
9. Gmail controls conversation grouping, pagination, read state, selection, and email actions.
10. Dock tabs and categorization controls remain available on label lists and their later pages.
11. Same-account navigation changes only the Gmail route. Different or unknown accounts use an account-qualified Gmail address.
12. Browser Back, Forward, and direct label navigation update the selected Dock tab.
13. The Inbox shortcut returns to Gmail's native inbox.
14. Outlook and standalone previews retain sample messages only.
15. Renaming the displayed category or migrating its older label keeps the same category open at its new Gmail address.
